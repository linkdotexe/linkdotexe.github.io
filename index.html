link.exe [freelink + tipp.exe]

link.exe is Jason Andrew Clark (NL) and Rheece van der Linde (ZA), a duo formed at 89 Sound Art School in Struer, Denmark, after landing on the same obsession with building instruments out of microcontrollers. We perform fully improvised, treating routing and feedback as the compositional material rather than working from a fixed piece.

Since meeting, we've developed the system across recorded sessions at Sound Art Lab in Struer and our debut show inside a Bang & Olufsen speaker testing facility, with radio performances in Amsterdam coming up. We return to Struer this October to teach Teensy Practices, the workshop that started this for both of us.
